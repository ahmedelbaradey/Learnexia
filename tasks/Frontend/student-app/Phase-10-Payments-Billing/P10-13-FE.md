## Scope for the frontend lead
A new **"Family Energy"** parent screen that visualizes the family wallet from P10-13-BE: the **two non-convertible buckets** (monthly subscription energy + permanent purchased reserve) at the top, and a **per-child allocation list** (each child's allocated / spent / remaining) below. This is a **read-only overview** — the only actions are buy-pack (deep-link to the existing P10-07 flow) and a "Redistribute" entry point **stubbed/disabled** until P10-14 lands. EN + AR, RTL-correct, parent-account only.

## Acceptance-criteria coverage (FE portion)
- Wallet's two **non-convertible** buckets (subscription temporary / purchased permanent) shown distinctly → **FE-2**
- Per-child allocation (allocated / spent / remaining), sums tie to the subscription bucket → **FE-3**
- Read-only overview; buy-pack deep-link; redistribution **stubbed** for P10-14 → **FE-4**
- Parent-only, no child surface → **FE-1** (guard) + **FE-6** (asserts child cannot reach it)
- EN+AR / RTL → **FE-5, FE-7**
- Allocation/spend correctness, idempotency, migration, the spend algorithm → **backend** (api-tester + security-auditor), not FE

## Contract from Backend (P10-13)
- `GET /api/Billing/FamilyEnergy/Overview` → `BaseResponse<FamilyEnergyOverviewDto>`:
  `{ subscriptionBalance, purchasedBalance, subscriptionExpiresAtUtc, children: [{ childId, allocated, spent, remaining }] }`
  — parent-JWT-only; family-scoped; success flag spelled **`Successed`**. Consume via the typed `api-client`.

## Notes
- **Read-only this story.** All mutation (sibling-to-sibling redistribution) is **P10-14** — render its entry point disabled so the screen is forward-compatible without shipping redistribution logic here.
- **No child surface** — children only ever see the energy meter (P10-10). The Family Energy screen is parent-account only; FE-1 guards the route and FE-6 asserts a child session cannot reach it.
- **Buy-pack** is the existing **P10-07** flow (purchased reserve is the **shared** family bucket, never per-child) — do not build a new checkout; deep-link.
- **Subscription vs purchased must read as two independent balances** — copy and layout must not imply convertibility (it is a hard backend rule).
- Reuse existing `packages/ui` primitives (`KPIStatCard`, `MasteryBar`, `GradientBox`) — **ask before any new design pattern** (CLAUDE.md rule 8).
- This screen complements the existing **P10-05** "Plan & billing" tab and the **P10-08** billing history; consider a cross-link, but keep wallet/allocation here.
