## Contract from Backend (P1-10-BE)
- Sign-In → `BaseResponse<JwtAuthResponse>`; `Me` → `role = Admin`.
- **P1-13 Sign-In hardening (FE-6):** locked account → distinct localized "account locked" `BaseResponse` message; failed sign-in → single generic "invalid credentials" (no user-not-found vs wrong-password distinction). FE must not branch on the two failure causes.
