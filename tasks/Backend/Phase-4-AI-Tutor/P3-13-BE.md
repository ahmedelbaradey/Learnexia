## Acceptance-criteria coverage

- **AC1 — Four derived attributes:** `StudentProfileEngine` derives question-type affinity, recurring-error clusters, attention-span/fatigue, `PreferredExplanationStyle` → **BE-4**
- **AC2 — Rule-based & explainable:** pure static engine, no ML, deterministic rules, recomputed on schedule + completion → **BE-4, BE-6, BE-7**
- **AC3 — Queryable & exposed:** `GET /api/Learning/Profile` + `IStudentProfileService.GetProfile` seam for P3-08/P3-03 → **BE-8, BE-6**

- **AC5 — Transparency & isolation:** inspection endpoint IDOR-scoped to JWT student; no other child's data; data-minimization enforced → **BE-8** + mandatory security-auditor gate
