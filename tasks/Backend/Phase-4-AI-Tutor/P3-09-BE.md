- **Backfill:** new table starts empty; students with prior attempts get mastery rows on their next completion (lazy fill). Flag if the lead wants a one-time backfill job.
- **P2-04 convergence:** P2-04's `LearningPathEngine` continues reading its transient `GetSkillMasteryForStudentInSubjectAsync` aggregate — do NOT migrate P2-04 in this story. Both use the same cumulative-accuracy formula so they won't silently diverge. A HANDOFF note is sufficient.
