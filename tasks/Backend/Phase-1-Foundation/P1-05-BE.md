## Acceptance-criteria coverage
- Wrong role → 403 → **BE-2, BE-5**
- Students can't access parent reports / other students; parents not learners; admin-only curriculum → **BE-2, BE-3**
- Secrets out of appsettings → **BE-4**
- Unauthenticated → 401 → **BE-5**
