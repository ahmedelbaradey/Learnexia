## Contract for downstream consumers
- `LessonCompletedIntegrationEvent(EventId, OccurredOnUtc, StudentId, LessonId, SkillId, AccuracyPercentage, CorrectAnswerCount)`.
- `AnswerSubmittedIntegrationEvent(EventId, OccurredOnUtc, StudentId, LessonId, SkillId, CorrectAnswerCount)`.
- New consumers register a sibling `INotificationHandler<>` — fan-out is automatic via `IsolatedNotificationPublisher`; no edits to existing handlers.
