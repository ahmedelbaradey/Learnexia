## Acceptance-criteria coverage

- Parent buys 1000-credit pack ($5) via P10-06 checkout seam, assigns to specific child → **BE-2** (`StartPackCheckoutCommand`)
- Successful payment: 1000 credits land in child's `PurchasedBalance`, never expire → **BE-3** (`Purchase` ledger row, `PurchasedBalance` increment)
- Purchase recorded as `Purchase` `CreditTransaction` with payment ref + idempotency → **BE-3**
- Failed payment grants no credits → **BE-3** (no-credit path)
- Pack size and price read from **`IGlobalSettingsProvider.PackSize`** / **`IGlobalSettingsProvider.PackPriceEgp`** (P10-12 enabler) → **BE-2**
- Children cannot purchase — parent-gated → **BE-2** + **BE-4** (parent-JWT-only `[Authorize]`)
- Pack assigned to parent-owned child only → **BE-2** (family-scope check)
