## Notes

- **Option C service-only (CONVENTIONS §7):** allocation, spend, migration, and overview logic all live in **services** (`Application/Abstractions` interface, `Infrastructure` impl). Handlers validate/authorize and call services only — **no `DbContext`/`DbSet`/`IQueryable`/repository in Application**, no EF exceptions surfaced from Application. Explicit transactions live in the service/Infrastructure (ADR 0001 — no UoW).
- **Hotspot rule (hard):** the shared `PurchasedBalance` row must stay cold — only the purchased-fallback branch in BE-7 touches it, so concurrent siblings contend on their **own** allocation rows. Do not read/lock the wallet on the normal allocation-covered spend path.
- **Non-convertible buckets (hard):** there is intentionally no method/endpoint to move energy between `SubscriptionBalance` and `PurchasedBalance`. Reviewers must reject any such path.
- **Seam reuse (OQ-6):** the AI module keeps calling `ICreditSpendService.TryDebitAsync(childId, …)` unchanged (P10-03) — **only the Billing impl changes**. No AI-module edits in this story.
- **Migration safety (BE-9):** idempotent, rerun-safe, reconciled against the preserved ledger before old paths retire. Coordinate the migration sequence with any other in-flight `billing`-schema migrations (PARALLELISM.md — serialized migration edits).
- **`PlanEnergyPerSeat` (BE-6):** the live seeded `credits.premium_monthly` (5000) / `credits.free_monthly` (100) settings are re-read as **per-seat** values via `IGlobalSettingsProvider` (P10-12) — update the GlobalSettings semantics/labels in coordination with P10-12. No hardcoded 5000/100 constants.
- **Forward-compat with the cluster:** `ChildEnergyAllocation` carries transfer methods (used by **P10-16** sibling redistribution) and `CycleStart`/`CycleEnd` for grace/enforcement (**P10-15**); refunds (**P10-09/P10-17**) reconcile against **purchased** only — never subscription. Keep these contracts stable. **Canonical cluster mapping: 13 wallet · 14 seats · 15 lifecycle (grace+enforcement) · 16 redistribution · 17 refunds.**
- **Security (money + child data):** every read/spend is family-scoped (owning-parent authz); no cross-family energy visibility; all movements idempotent + ledgered; wallet funding only from verified provider webhooks (P10-06/07). `security-auditor` gate on this batch — Critical/High block.
- **Blocked by P10-01** (ledger + Billing module + spend seam), **P10-02** (grant job), and **P10-14** (seats / `ActivePaidSeats`). Feeds **P10-16** (redistribution) and **P10-15** (grace/enforcement), reconciled by **P10-09/P10-17** (refunds). **Canonical mapping: 13 wallet · 14 seats · 15 lifecycle · 16 redistribution · 17 refunds.**
