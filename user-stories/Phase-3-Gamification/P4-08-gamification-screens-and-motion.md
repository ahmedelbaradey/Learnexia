# Gamification screens & motion

- **Project:** Learnexia
- **Sprint / Phase:** Phase 3 — Gamification (Week 5)
- **Epic:** Gamification Screens
- **Issue type:** Story
- **Story Points:** 5 — several screens plus the motion/animation spec that sells the reward loop.
- **Labels:** `frontend`, `gamification`, `motion`
- **Requirements:** FR-GM, NFR-6, NFR-7

## Description
As a student, I want satisfying reward, badge, league, and mission screens with delightful motion, so that earning progress feels exciting and keeps me coming back.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Screens exist for: Reward, Badge collection, League, Missions, and Hearts/Practice mode.
- Motion specs are implemented: XP fill, badge pop-in, confetti, shake on wrong answer, animated flame.
- Animations respect kid-accessibility (clear visual feedback on every action) and perform smoothly on mobile.
- Screens render in Arabic (RTL) and English.
