# Pipeline Brief — P10-04: Daily soft cap & low-energy warning

> **Phase 10 credit economy — pacing + warning layer.** A per-child, per-day **soft cap** (Free = 10, Premium = 250, config-driven) drawn from the monthly pool, with a friendly warning on reaching it (the child may continue while monthly balance remains — a configurable hard-stop is **off** by default), plus a **low-energy warning** at a threshold, and a **timezone-aware daily reset** at the child's local midnight. **Depends on P10-01** (the credit account it reads against) and consumes **P10-11** config. The warning copy is surfaced by the kid energy UI (**P10-10**); this story owns the **backend** counter + thresholds + reset. Read `docs/briefs/P10-01.md` and `docs/briefs/P10-11.md`.

## Summary & traceability

## Business context & value

- **Who benefits:** the **child** (a friendly nudge to pace use instead of a jarring cut-off — protects the learning experience and teaches self-regulation), the **parent** (children don't burn the whole month's energy in one sitting; predictable consumption), and the **business** (daily caps smooth AI-cost spikes and reduce the chance a single heavy day exhausts the monthly grant prematurely, generating support load).
- **"Soft" is the whole point (lead-confirmed):** the cap is a **warning + pacing signal**, not a wall. On reaching the daily cap the child sees *"you've used today's recommended energy — this is part of your monthly limit"* and **may continue** as long as monthly balance remains. A **hard-stop is configurable but OFF by default** — the default behavior never blocks a motivated learner. This aligns with FR-AI-6 / the AI-Helper "never block learning" invariant.
- **Bounded by the monthly pool (lead-confirmed):** the daily cap is a *recommendation within* the monthly grant, not an independent allowance. The child can't spend more in a month than the monthly pool regardless of daily caps; the daily cap just paces it. So the daily counter is informational/threshold-driving, while the **monthly balance (P10-01) is the real ceiling**.
- **Low-energy warning** is the early-heads-up so a child (and, via P10-10/parent surfaces, a parent) isn't surprised when energy runs low — it fires before the insufficient-balance decline (P10-03) ever triggers, giving room to slow down or for the parent to top up (P10-07).
- **Success measure (from AC):** a per-child-per-day counter tracks daily usage; reaching the cap shows the soft warning and allows continuation (default); the low-energy warning fires at the threshold; the counter resets at the child's local midnight.

## Current-state findings (reality in code)

1. **Timezone-aware daily reset has a direct precedent** — Gamification's streak system computes "today" via a configured `TimeZoneId` + `ISystemClock` (`StreakDayCalculator.Today(timeZoneId, clock)`, used in `StreakSweepJob`). **Reuse this pattern** for the daily-cap reset. The story wants the **child's local midnight** (per-child timezone) — which is finer-grained than the single platform `TimeZoneId` the streak system uses (OQ-1 — per-child tz vs platform tz).
2. **The daily counter store is a design choice (OQ-2).** Options: (a) a `CreditAccount` column pair `(DailyUsed, DailyUsedDateLocal)` reset lazily on read/spend when the local date rolls; (b) a small `DailyUsageCounter` row per child-per-day; (c) a Redis counter (Redis is in the stack per HANDOFF.md — used for Gamification leaderboards/cache). **Recommend the lazy-reset column pair on `CreditAccount`** (no new table, reset is a cheap compare-and-zero on the next spend/read — mirrors how the streak system lazily detects day rollover). Confirm.
3. **P10-01 owns the monthly pool** — P10-04 reads `GrantedBalance + PurchasedBalance` and `GrantExpiresAtUtc` from the `CreditAccount`; the daily counter is *additional* state, not a second pool. P10-04 must not create a parallel balance.
4. **P10-03 increments the daily counter** — the natural increment point is the same delivery-time debit in P10-03 (`+cost` to the daily counter when energy is spent). So P10-04 exposes an increment + threshold-check that P10-03's spend path calls, or P10-04's logic is read off the ledger's same-day `Spend` rows (OQ-3 — counter column vs derive-from-ledger).
5. **No "soft cap" or daily-usage concept exists anywhere** — greenfield. The closest analog is the MVP daily-*request*-count guardrail (`ai-cost-routing.md §6`), which P10-03 removes; P10-04's daily-*energy* soft cap is its conceptual successor but is a warning, not a gate.

## Acceptance criteria (testable)

1. **AC-1 (daily soft cap tracked per child per day):** A counter tracks energy used per child per local day; the cap is Free = 10, Premium = 250 (**from P10-11 config**, by plan tier). The cap is **bounded by / drawn from the monthly pool** (not an independent allowance).
2. **AC-2 (soft semantics):** On reaching the daily cap, the system surfaces a **warning** (copy: it's part of the monthly limit) and **allows continuation** while monthly balance remains. A **hard-stop is configurable and OFF by default** — default behavior does not block.
3. **AC-3 (low-energy warning):** A low-energy warning fires at a configured threshold — e.g. ≤ 10% of monthly remaining, or near the daily cap (exact rule OQ-4). The warning is a **signal/state** P10-10 surfaces, not a block.
4. **AC-4 (timezone-aware reset):** The daily counter resets at the **child's local midnight** (timezone-aware). At reset, the daily-used count returns to 0; the monthly pool is untouched (only the monthly grant/expiry, P10-02, touches the pool).
5. **AC-5 (conventions):** Backend returns `BaseResponse<T>` / `Successed` where a query/command is exposed; `ILoggerManager`; `ISystemClock` + configured timezone for "today"; reads the monthly pool from P10-01 (no parallel balance); cap/threshold values from P10-11 config; lives in `Billing`.

## Affected modules & data

- **State (OQ-2 — recommend lazy-reset columns, no new table):** add to `CreditAccount` (P10-01): `DailyUsed` (int), `DailyUsedDateLocal` (date) — on each spend/read, if `DailyUsedDateLocal != child's local today`, zero `DailyUsed` and set the date (lazy reset, mirrors streak day-rollover detection). **Alternative:** a `DailyUsageCounter` row or a Redis counter (OQ-2). If columns are added to `CreditAccount`, that's a **schema change coordinated with P10-01** — ideally folded into the P10-01 migration if sequencing allows, else an additive P10-04 migration.
- **NEW read seam / signal:** an `EnergyStatusQuery` (or extend P10-01's `GetCreditAccountQuery`) returning `{ MonthlyBalance, DailyUsed, DailyCap, DailyCapReached, LowEnergy, GrantExpiresAtUtc }` — the shape **P10-10** renders and **P10-03** consults for the decline copy. **Recommend this is the single read seam both P10-10 and P10-04 logic share.**
- **NEW config (P10-11):** per-tier `DailyCap`, the `LowEnergyThreshold` rule, the `HardStopEnabled` flag (default false). Consumed from the `BillingConfig` store.
- **MODIFIED (P10-03 interlock):** P10-03's spend path increments `DailyUsed` (or P10-04 derives daily-used from same-day `Spend` ledger rows — OQ-3). If column-based, the increment is part of the same atomic debit transaction (so daily-used and the ledger never diverge).
- **Existing entities:** none beyond the `CreditAccount` column additions.

## Handoff → db-migration

- **If OQ-2 = columns on `CreditAccount` (recommended):** add `DailyUsed` (int, default 0) + `DailyUsedDateLocal` (date, nullable) to the `CreditAccount` config. **Coordinate with P10-01** — if P10-04 is built right after P10-01, fold these into a single additive migration or add a small `AddDailyCapTracking` migration in the `billing` schema. No cross-module FK, manual-apply per CONVENTIONS.
- **If OQ-2 = separate `DailyUsageCounter` table:** `ChildId` (loose int), `LocalDate` (date), `EnergyUsed` (int), unique on `(ChildId, LocalDate)`; index for the current-day lookup. (More rows, explicit history; recommend the column approach unless daily history is wanted.)
- **If OQ-2 = Redis:** **no migration** — counter in Redis keyed `dailyenergy:{childId}:{localDate}` with a TTL to midnight. (Fast, ephemeral; loses durable daily history — fine since the ledger is the durable record.)

## Handoff → backend-feature

- **Daily reset (lazy, mirror streak day-rollover):** a helper that, given the child's timezone + `ISystemClock`, computes the local "today"; on any spend/read, if the stored `DailyUsedDateLocal` is stale, zero `DailyUsed` and stamp today **before** evaluating the cap. No scheduled job required if lazy (recommended); a Hangfire reset sweep is an alternative for cleanliness but unnecessary (OQ-5).
- **Cap + threshold evaluation:** compute `DailyCapReached = DailyUsed >= DailyCap` and `LowEnergy = monthlyRemaining <= threshold` (OQ-4 rule). These are **flags/signals**, not gates — return them in the status read; **do not block** unless `HardStopEnabled` is true (default false) and the daily cap is reached.
- **Increment seam (P10-03 interlock):** expose an `IncrementDailyUsage(childId, amount)` the P10-03 debit calls within its transaction (column approach), **or** compute daily-used by summing same-local-day `Spend` ledger rows (derive approach, OQ-3). **Recommend the column increment inside the debit transaction** (cheap, atomic, no per-read aggregation).
- **`EnergyStatusQuery` (shared read for P10-10 + P10-03):** returns the full status object above. **Read-only.** This is the contract P10-10 renders and P10-03 consults — define it here so both consume one shape.
- **Warning copy:** the **threshold logic** lives here; the **copy/localization (AR+EN)** is surfaced by P10-10. P10-04 returns a typed warning state (`None` / `DailyCapReached` / `LowEnergy`), not localized strings — P10-10 maps state → copy. Confirm this split (OQ-6).
- **Conventions:** `BaseResponse<T>` / `Successed`; `ILoggerManager`; `ISystemClock` + timezone; config from P10-11; no parallel balance (reads the P10-01 pool).

## Handoff → security-auditor (light gate)

- **Soft cap cannot be bypassed to overspend the monthly pool:** confirm the daily cap is informational and the **monthly pool (P10-01) remains the real ceiling** — a child hitting the daily cap and continuing still cannot spend below zero (P10-03's insufficient-balance path catches that). No way to "reset" the daily counter early to dodge the warning into free spend (the monthly ceiling is independent of the daily counter).
- **Timezone manipulation:** if the child's timezone is client-supplied, confirm a child can't spoof their timezone to repeatedly trigger "local midnight" resets of the daily counter to dodge the cap — but since the cap is *soft* (no block) and the monthly pool is the real limit, the blast radius is low. **Recommend** the timezone is derived server-side (from the child's profile, set at onboarding) not client-sent (OQ-1). Flag if client-sent.
- **No PII:** the daily counter is amounts + dates + loose child id only.
- **Not a hard gate** (no money moves in P10-04; it's a warning/counter) — light pass.

## Handoff → api-tester

- Spend below the cap → `DailyCapReached = false`, no warning.
- Spend up to the cap (Free: 10) → `DailyCapReached = true`, warning state returned, **and the child can still spend** (soft, hard-stop off) while monthly balance remains.
- Hard-stop ON (config flip) + cap reached → spend declines (verify the configurable hard-stop works when enabled).
- Low-energy threshold: drive monthly remaining ≤ threshold → `LowEnergy = true`.
- Daily reset: advance the clock past the child's local midnight (inject `ISystemClock`) → `DailyUsed` resets to 0 on the next read/spend; the monthly pool is unchanged.
- Config-driven: change the cap in P10-11 → the new cap applies (no redeploy).
- Monthly ceiling: even past the daily cap, spending stops at monthly balance = 0 (insufficient → P10-03 graceful decline).

- **OQ-3 (increment vs derive):** increment a `DailyUsed` column inside P10-03's debit transaction (**recommended** — atomic, cheap), or derive daily-used by summing same-local-day `Spend` ledger rows on each read (no extra state, but per-read aggregation + timezone-bucketing complexity)? Confirm.
- **OQ-4 (low-energy threshold rule):** the story says *"≤ 10% of monthly remaining, or near the daily cap"* — pick the exact rule(s) and whether both fire. **Recommend: low-energy = monthly remaining ≤ 10% of the monthly allotment** (simple, plan-relative), with the daily-cap-reached warning as a separate state. Product decision — confirm the percentages.
- **OQ-5 (reset mechanism):** lazy reset on next spend/read (**recommended** — no job, mirrors streak rollover) vs a Hangfire daily reset sweep (cleaner state but an extra job and timezone-fan-out complexity). **Recommend lazy.** Confirm.
- **OQ-6 (warning state vs copy):** P10-04 returns a typed warning **state** and P10-10 owns the localized **copy** (AR+EN) — **recommended** (keeps localization in the UI layer). Or does P10-04 return ready-made copy? Confirm the split so P10-10's designer knows what to render.
- **OQ-7 (hard-stop config scope):** the configurable hard-stop is per-plan, global, or per-child? **Recommend a global config flag (default OFF)** via P10-11 for MVP; per-plan/per-child later. Confirm.

### Assumptions
- P10-01 is merged; P10-04 may add 2 columns to `CreditAccount` (coordinated migration).
- The monthly pool (P10-01) is the real spend ceiling; the daily cap is a soft pacing signal within it.
- P10-11 (or config defaults) provides the per-tier daily cap, the threshold rule, and the hard-stop flag.
- P10-10 renders the `EnergyStatusQuery` output and owns the warning copy/localization.

### Risks (top 3)
- **R1 — timezone correctness** (local-midnight reset across DST / per-child tz) is fiddly. **Mitigation:** reuse the proven `StreakDayCalculator` timezone pattern; server-side tz; api-tester clock-advance case.
- **R2 — daily counter diverging from the ledger** (if column-incremented outside the debit transaction). **Mitigation:** increment inside P10-03's atomic debit, or derive from the ledger (OQ-3).
- **R3 — soft-cap misread as a hard block** (a bug that blocks by default) breaks the "never block learning" invariant. **Mitigation:** hard-stop OFF by default; explicit api-tester case that at-cap still allows spend.
