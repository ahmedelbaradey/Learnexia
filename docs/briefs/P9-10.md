# Pipeline Brief — P9-10 Kid-facing energy UI (⚡ طاقة المساعد)

> Phase 9 — Payment, Billing & Credits. The **only** student-app billing surface — and it is **READ-ONLY**. A child sees their Helper Energy (⚡ طاقة المساعد), per-action costs, a spend animation, and a friendly low/out-of-energy state that routes to **"ask a parent."** **The child NEVER sees a payment, purchase, or plan UI.** **Needs a `designer` stage.**

**Upstream dependencies:** **P9-03** (spend-energy on AI help — the charge-on-delivery flow + per-action costs the child sees) — **blocks P9-10**. **P9-01** (the `CreditAccount` balance this meter reads). **P9-11** (per-action costs config — hint 1 / explain-mistake 3 / deep-explanation 5 / similar-example (practice) 5). **P3-01/P3-04/P3-05** (the AI Helper actions whose costs are displayed). **P4-04** (gamification hearts — the thing this must be visually distinct from).

## Business context & value

- **Who benefits:** the **child** (understands what AI help costs, paces themselves, never hits a hard wall — out-of-energy gracefully routes to a grown-up) and the **parent/business** (the "ask a parent" state is the funnel into P9-05/06/07 top-ups).
- **Value:** makes the credit economy legible + kid-appropriate. The hard requirements are **(1) visual distinction from hearts** (P4-04 hearts = lives/wrong-answer mechanic; energy = AI-help currency — confusing them breaks both mental models), **(2) cost transparency before confirming an action**, and **(3) the read-only + "ask a parent" boundary** (no purchase UI ever reaches a child).
- **Success measured by:** a child can read their energy, sees each action's cost before confirming, watches energy decrement on a successful charge, and on empty gets a friendly "refills monthly / ask a parent" state — all in AR (RTL) + EN, never seeing the word "Credits."

---

## Acceptance criteria (testable)

1. An **energy meter shows the current balance**, **visually distinct from gamification hearts (P4-04)** — different **icon (⚡ vs ♥), colour, and placement** so a child never confuses energy with lives/hearts.
2. Each Helper action shows its **energy cost BEFORE the child confirms**: **hint ⚡1, explain-mistake ⚡3, deep-explanation ⚡5, similar-example ⚡5** (costs read from config / the P9-03 cost source, not hard-coded).
3. A **spend animation** plays when energy is charged (on successful delivery, consistent with P9-03 charge-on-delivery — no charge shown on refusal/error/insufficient).
4. A friendly **low-energy** and **out-of-energy** state explains **when it refills (monthly)** and offers **"ask a parent"** — **children cannot purchase**; there is **no payment/plan/purchase UI** anywhere in the child app.
5. Full **RTL/Arabic + English**; **kid-appropriate copy**; the word **"Credits" is NEVER shown to the child** — always **"طاقة" / "energy."**
6. The meter reflects the **dual-pool** balance as a single kid-facing number (granted + purchased shown as one "energy" total — the child does not see the pool split; confirm in OQ).

## Affected modules & data

- **Frontend only (student-app / child area)** — no new backend in this story. It **consumes existing reads**:
  - **Current energy balance** — a `GET` from the credit-core (P9-01/P9-03), returning the child's spendable energy (granted + purchased as one number, plus possibly refill date). **Confirm this endpoint exists from P9-01/P9-03** — if not, flag a tiny BE read as a dependency (mirror the NSwag-gap pattern from `W12-P2-05-06-07-FE.md` §4.4).
  - **Per-action costs** — from P9-11 config / the P9-03 cost table (hint 1 / explain-mistake 3 / deep-explanation 5 / similar-example 5). Likely surfaced via the AI-Helper action endpoints (P3-05 etc.) or a small config read.
  - **Refill date / monthly reset** — the child's `GrantedBalance` expiry/cycle date (P9-02), shown as "refills on …" in the empty state.
- **No new entities, no migration.** The energy balance + cost reads are owned upstream.

## Handoff → frontend

- **Surface:** the **child area** of the Expo universal app — the energy meter in the **lesson/quiz header** (alongside but visually distinct from hearts) and on the AI-Helper action affordances; plus the low/out-of-energy state.
- **api-client hooks:** `useEnergyBalance(childId/self)` (read the current energy + refill date — **confirm the endpoint**) and a cost source (`useActionCosts` or static-from-config; mirror how P9-03/P3-05 expose costs). If the read endpoints lack typed responses (NSwag `Promise<void>` gap, as in `W12-P2-05-06-07-FE.md` §4.4), resolve with `[ProducesResponseType]` + regen (recommended) or hand-rolled types — flag for the lead.
- **Primitives (new in `@learnexia/ui`):** `EnergyMeter` (⚡, distinct colour/placement from `Hearts`), `EnergyCostPill` (⚡N on an action button), `OutOfEnergyState` (refill date + "ask a parent"), spend-animation wrapper. **Reuse** existing token/RTL patterns; **do NOT** reuse the `Hearts` primitive's visual.
- **Rules:** **tokens only**, **i18n only** (EN+AR), **RTL via `useLocale().direction`**, logical positions (start/end). **CLAUDE.md rule 8** — a plain component + state is the right shape; no animation library / state machine pattern without asking. **The "ask a parent" CTA must not call any payment/checkout endpoint** — it is informational only (and children have no purchase capability). **Never render "Credits."**
- **Charge consistency:** the spend animation fires only on a **successful** AI-Helper delivery (P9-03 charge-on-delivery); on refuse/error/insufficient, **no charge animation** — instead show the low/out-of-energy state. The FE trusts the P9-03 response for whether a charge occurred.

## Open questions / assumptions / risks

Confirm the copy ("part of your energy refills monthly") vs simplifying to "refills monthly."
3. **"Ask a parent" behaviour:** purely informational copy (recommended — no deep-link, no notification), OR does it **notify the parent** / deep-link the parent app to buy a pack (P9-07)? A notify-the-parent relay is a nice funnel but adds a backend hook. **Recommend informational-only for v1; flag the relay as a follow-up.** Confirm with lead.
4. **Refill date source:** the child's `GrantedBalance` cycle/expiry from P9-02 — confirm it's readable for the empty-state copy.
5. **Visual-distinction sign-off:** the designer must get explicit confirmation that the energy meter reads as clearly different from hearts (P4-04 `18-hearts.png`) — this is an AC, not a nicety. Recommend a quick design review against the hearts capture.
6. **Where the meter lives:** lesson/quiz header (alongside hearts), home, and/or the AI-Helper panel — confirm placement set with the designer.
