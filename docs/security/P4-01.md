## Verdict: **PASS-with-notes**

All four prior findings (1 Critical, 1 High, 2 Medium) are resolved. No Critical or High remains. Two Low items and three Info notes only — none block the reviewer gate.

## Notes / accepted risks

- **Residual enumerable-id risk (prior High #2):** now acceptably mitigated. The opaque `Id` is the only identifier in the response body, and the by-`recipientUserId` lookup is reachable only by Admin/SuperAdmin behind `AdminOnly`. The lead's choice of admin-only (rather than current-user scoping) is reasonable for an admin/observability lookup; the endpoint is not exposed to students/parents.
- **Lead action item (Low #1):** the casing mismatch means the policy currently denies all callers. This is safe (fail-closed) but the endpoint will not function for admins until role-name casing is reconciled. Verify with a real admin JWT before closing the story. This is a correctness bug, flagged here because it sits on the security-control path.
- Producer publishes after commit and isolates publish failures (`AddUserCommandHandler.cs:153-158`); consumer is idempotent (`AnyAsync` guard). No change.
- No hardcoded secrets, no JWT secret/CORS/transport changes in this batch. (Note: `RequireHttpsMetadata = false` at `DependencyInjection.cs:117` is a pre-existing dev-only setting, out of scope for this batch — flag for prod hardening elsewhere.)

Security: PASS (with notes) — 0 blocking findings.
